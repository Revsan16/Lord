Introduction

Why this book

Christians are called to live a supernaturally successful life. Ignorance is one of the devil's most powerful weapons.

God has placed within every Christian the ability to live a supernatural life. Adam was created in God's image and lost his divine position through sin. Jesus restored that life through His death and resurrection. This book will awaken your spiritual man and transform your mind.

Contents

Seven chapters

  1. 01

    Supernatural Knowledge

    Faith is the victory that overcomes the world.

    The divine nature of faith, its power to transcend the five senses, and how it connects us to God's supernatural realm. It explains the difference between belief and faith, and how faith must be active, bold, and rooted in the finished work of the cross.

  2. 02

    Christianity Is Deeper

    Deep calls to deep.

    A challenge to the shallow practices of modern Christianity, and a call to pursue profound spiritual experience, divine encounter, and a life beyond religious ritual.

  3. 03

    Seeking Eternal Solutions

    The unseen world created the seen world.

    The eternal principles of God's kingdom, the reality of spiritual warfare, and how divine laws and mysteries offer permanent solutions to the challenges of life.
