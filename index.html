About our ministry

Prophet T. Vinoth Kumar

Founder · International New Life Prophetic Ministries

He has walked with God for more than 25 years. From a young age he obeyed God's call to serve Him.

  • We conduct pastors' training and conferences across many states of India.
  • He carries a strong prophetic anointing for this end time.
  • He explores the scripture and reveals the truth with deep insight into end-time revelation. God has entrusted him with the gift of prophecy, and uses him to influence and uplift countless lives.
  • He has written As You Think, You Become and Keys of Successful Life (English & Tamil).

He is one of the finest teachers at revealing the depths and breaking open the ground truths of the scriptures. His teaching helps many people know the true nature of God.
