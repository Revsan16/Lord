Our pastor

Prophet T. Vinoth Kumar

He has walked with God for more than 25 years, and has served this city and much of India since he was a young man.

  • Trains pastors and holds conferences across many states of India.
  • Carries a strong prophetic anointing for this end time.
  • Opens the scriptures with deep insight into end-time revelation.
  • Author of As You Think, You Become and Keys of Successful Life.

His teaching helps people know the true nature of God — not as an idea, but as something they can live in.
