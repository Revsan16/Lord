1

God Reached Man

Man did nothing to reach God, but God did everything to reach man!

Read

Salvation is not a human plan. The relationship between God and man was disconnected because of man's sin. Sin had a legal claim on mankind. Satan bound all man and made them his slave. Man made no effort to reach God. The man never bothered to be delivered from his own sin. He lived in his own ways, which led his life to death.

Romans 5:5–10
2

Agape Love

Man’s sin cannot change God’s divine love! But, the divine love of God can change the sin of the whole world..

Read

People often believe that their sins affect God's love for them. This is truly a wrong thought. Because sin does not affect God, but it affects man. When man sins, he gives place to the devil. Satan, who is looking for someone to devour, enters his life and destroys him. God loves man more than anything else, so He is concerned about the well-being of man.

3

Connot Labour the Favour

Grace is not license to sin! Grace is the strength to overcome sin!

Read

Grace is often misunderstood and also, people try to earn God’s favour. Grace is not grace if it is earned. Because grace is a free gift from God. It cannot be earned by man. Do not misunderstand grace. Do not think that it encourages sin. That is completely wrong.

For example, we insure our vehicle. We do not let our vehicle get into an accident just because we have insurance. In case of an accident, we can use the insurance. Similarly, grace is not a license to sin, it is the strength to overcome your weakness.

Beloved!

His grace is sufficient for your successful life.

Rev 6:1,2,14

Faith

Faith is not believing that God can do great things for us! Faith is believing that God has already finished everything on the cross!

Prayer is born of faith. Faith is born from what, God has done on the cross. Faith is not a superstitious belief. Faith comes from hearing the word of God, believing it, confessing it, and doing according to that word. God has earned everything on the cross that man needs. Man's life begins on the finished work of Christ on Cross. We begin, where God finished. <p class="fw-bold">Dear friends!

Believe! Receive God's blessings in your life.

Beloved!

Repentance comes through the love and goodness of God.

Behavior! Thoughts!

Fruit is the result of the root! Likewise, our behaviour is the result of our thoughts!

Read

Our actions are the xerox copy of our mind. Without knowing this many people focus on their behaviours. They sincerely try to change their behaviours and finally they are disappointed and exhausted. They were sincere. But they were sincerely wrong. Our behaviours are the results of our thoughts. Good fruits are the result of the root. Likewise, good behaviours are the result of good thoughts. Wise people focus on their thought life. <p class="fw-bold">Beloved!

Read and meditate on the word of God, which transforms our minds.

Proverbs 23:7, Luke 6:43, Romans 12:1

Laziness

Laziness is a slow poison!

Read

Success is the result of hard work and effective work. Laziness kills success little by little. God cannot use a man who is lazy. Procrastination is a sign of laziness. Analyse your life and get rid of it.

Proverbs 12:24, 27; 13:4, 6:6-10
22

Self-pity is Pride

Self-pity is the root of pride!

Read

Self-pity prevents us from experiencing God's grace. It is a sign of self-centeredness, which distances us from God and people. Kill self-pity before it kills your faith and reliance on God.

Luke 10:38-43
23

Excuses!

Excuses stop you from moving forward!

Read

Excuses are a barrier to success. Opportunity does not return. Stability and consistency overcome excuses. Success is in your hands; don’t compromise or miss God’s best.

John 5:1-8
24

Does Prayer Bring Success?

Prayer brings good results, not success alone!

Read

Prayer does not directly create success. It equips us with wisdom, knowledge, and strength to achieve what God desires for our lives. Effective prayer leads to a fruitful Christian life.

Matthew 6:6-8
25

Person! Principle!

God’s kingdom operates on principles, not persons!

Read

Success depends on understanding and following God’s principles. God evaluates hearts, not faces. Even biblical examples like Cain and Abel show that principles guide God’s acceptance.

Genesis 4:7, 4:2-12
26

God's Timing! Man's Timing!

God acts according to His plan, not our timing!

Read

God’s timing is perfect. We must learn patience and trust His plan, as exemplified by Zechariah, Elizabeth, and Mary. Waiting is an investment, not wasted time.

Luke 1:28,11-15

Waiting

Waiting time is investing time!

Read

Isaiah 40:31

Humble

Humility is an attitude and intention!

Read

True humility comes from mind and intention, not outward behavior. Recognizing others as greater and valuing lives is the essence of being humble.

James 4:6

Religion? Christianity?

Christianity is about relationship, not religion!

Read

Jesus gave His life to build a relationship with man, not to establish rules or laws. Experience God’s love and liberty rather than following religious rituals.

John 10:1-15

Longing Heart

Love for God begins with a longing heart!

Read

God values a heart that longs for Him, not perfection. Love sincerely from your heart, even in imperfection.

Psalm 91:14, 42:1

Compete! Complete!

Life is about completing your purpose, not competing!

Read

Competing or comparing creates envy and hatred. Focus on fulfilling God’s plan for your life, just like David and Jesus completed their purpose.

1 Samuel 18:6-15

Rich! Poor!

The poorest person is one without purpose!

Read

Wealth without purpose is poverty. Moses discovered his purpose when God met him in the burning bush. Purpose makes life meaningful and successful.

Proverbs 3:1-10, 29:18

Money

Money having you is a sin!

Read

Money itself is not evil, but being controlled by it leads to destruction. Handle riches wisely, stay thankful, and focus on God.

Proverbs 22:7, 10:22
34

Smart Work! Over Work!

Work smart, don’t be a workaholic!

Read

Stressful overwork is destructive. God designed a balanced life with time for God, work, and relationships. Smart work brings success, not exhaustion.

Acts 5:1-10
35

Curse

Only the blood of Jesus delivers from curse!

Read

Curse is Satan’s tool to bring negative results. Believers are protected through faith in Jesus. Without God, curse produces sin, sickness, and poverty.

Deuteronomy 28:15-29; John 10:10

Fear

Kill fear before it kills you!

Read

Fear controls and enslaves. David defeated Goliath because he overcame fear. Fear must be recognized and conquered to achieve success.

1 John 4:18

Fearing Sin! Hating Sin!

Conscious love of God leads to hating sin!

Read

Fear of punishment leads to bondage. God’s love empowers freedom from sin. Hate sin out of love for God, just like Joseph avoided Potiphar’s wife.

1 John 4:16-19

The Storm

Storms carry you to where you should be!

Read

Storms are allowed to move you forward, teach, and strengthen. They are opportunities to fulfill God’s purpose, as seen in David and Paul’s lives.

Acts 27:14-26

Giving to God

Giving is a sign of love!

Read

Giving should come from the heart, not the amount. God appreciates sincere giving, even in lack, as shown by the poor woman’s offering.

Mark 12:41-44

Big Heart

Big dreams require a big heart!

Read

Your heart determines how much you can give and receive. Joseph had a big dream and a big heart to bless others. Expand your heart to achieve spiritual success.

Genesis 45:4-18

Love! War!

God loves you; that’s why Satan is at war with you!

Read

Problems often indicate God’s blessing and love. Enemies attack because God’s favor is upon us, like Joseph facing his brothers’ jealousy.

Genesis 37:3-4
42

Leader!

A leader is one who leads the way!

Read

Leadership is earned through trust, honesty, and commitment. Jesus led by example, not just by showing the way.

1 Corinthians 11:1, 1 Peter 2:21
43

Leader! Successor!

Leadership begins with followers and ends with successors!

Read

Successful leaders raise successors. Jesus trained the disciples, who then carried His vision to the world.

Matthew 28:18-20, Mark 3:13-15

Connection

Better to be alone than in bad company!

Read

Connections impact life. Good relationships bring blessings; bad ones bring harm. Divine connections help achieve success, like Joseph with the chief butler.

Genesis 41:8-14, 1 Samuel 18:1-3

People care about your heart, not your knowledge!

Read

Spiritual life is measured by care, not intelligence. Jesus cared, which saved lives. Big heart comes before brain.

Don’t judge when God’s hand is upon someone!

Read

John 18:25-27, 21:15-17

Read

Read
