About the School of Daniel

Know God. Hear Him. Demonstrate His power.

School of Daniel is the training school of INLPM. It runs six schools that raise believers to know God, hear Him, and demonstrate His power.

Our mission
  1. 01

    Train

    Train every believer as a soldier of Christ, grounded in the Word and in prayer.

  2. 02

    Equip

    Equip believers with the gifts and power of the Holy Spirit for spiritual warfare.

  3. 03

    Raise

    Raise ministers, prophets, healers and leaders to carry the end-time revival.

  4. 04

    Prepare

    Prepare the Body of Christ in holiness, unity and readiness for the end-time harvest.

  5. 05

    Send

    Send trained believers into churches and nations to ignite and sustain revival.

Our schools

Six schools, one path

Each school has its own focus. School of Ministry is the 2-week foundation programme, and Deeper Realms is the advanced school.

  1. I

    School of Ministry Foundation

    Foundations for every minister: Word, prayer, preaching, character

    2 weeks
  2. II

    School of Prophets

    Hearing God, the prophetic gift, demonstrating power

    To be confirmed
  3. III

    School of Healing

    Faith for healing, laying on of hands, ministering to the sick

    To be confirmed
  4. IV

    School of Leadership

    Servant leadership, vision, teams, raising successors

    To be confirmed
  5. V

    Apostolic and Prophetic Vision

    Five-fold ministry, building churches, reaching nations

    To be confirmed
  6. VI

    Deeper Realms Advanced

    Intimacy, consecration, revelation, the presence of God

    To be confirmed

I

School I of VI · Foundation

School of Ministry

A 2-week foundation programme for everyone called to serve. It builds the Word, prayer and character every minister needs.

Duration
2 weeks
Who should attend
Believers sensing a call to ministry, church workers, youth leaders, worship team members and cell group leaders.

Modules

4 modules
  1. 01

    The Call of God

    Recognising, confirming and responding to the call to ministry.

  2. 02

    The Value of Salvation

    Salvation, the Word, the Holy Spirit and the Church.

  3. 03

    The Importance of Prayer

    Building a daily prayer life that carries ministry.

  4. 04

    Character of a Minister

    Integrity, humility, purity and accountability.

II

School II of VI

School of Prophets

We do not speak power. We demonstrate power.

This school trains students to hear God accurately and to move in the prophetic with maturity.

Duration
To be confirmed

Modules

8 modules
  1. 01

    Depth of the Holy Spirit

    Knowing the Holy Spirit as a Person, and moving from acquaintance to intimacy.

  2. 02

    Supernatural Language

    Praying in the Spirit and the place of tongues in a prophetic life.

    1 Corinthians 14
  3. 03

    Life Beyond Senses

    Living by faith, not by sight.

    2 Corinthians 5:7
  4. 04

    Hearing God’s Voice

    How God speaks through the Word, the inner witness, dreams and visions, and how to test what you hear.

    John 10:27
  5. 05

    Understanding the Spiritual Realm

    The seen and the unseen, angels, spiritual warfare and the authority of the believer.

    2 Corinthians 4:18
  6. 06

    Seven Dimensions

    The sevenfold Spirit: the Spirit of the Lord, wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge and the fear of the Lord.

    Isaiah 11:2
  7. 07

    Dealing with the Gift of Prophecy

    Accuracy, timing, protocol and ethics; judging prophecy and staying accountable.

    1 Corinthians 14:29 · 1 Thessalonians 5:20–21
  8. 08

    Five Levels of Understanding

    Growing from knowledge to understanding, wisdom, revelation and obedient application.

To be confirmed

8 modules

  3. 03

    The Healing Ministry of Jesus

  4. 04

    Faith for Healing

    Inner Healing and Deliverance

School IV of VI

School of Leadership

Then this Daniel was preferred above the presidents and princes, because an excellent spirit was in him. Daniel 6:3

To be confirmed

Modules

8 modules
  1. 01

    Biblical Foundations of Leadership

    Servant leadership as Jesus modelled it.

    Mark 10:43–45
  2. 02

    Character Before Charisma

    Integrity, humility and faithfulness when no one is watching.

    Daniel 6:4
  3. 03

    Vision and Direction

    Receiving vision from God and making it plain.

    Habakkuk 2:2
  4. 04

    Building and Leading Teams

    Delegation and sharing the load.

    Exodus 18:17–23
  5. 05

    Raising Disciples and Successors

    Training faithful people who will train others.

    2 Timothy 2:2
  6. 06

    Stewardship and Administration

    Faithful handling of finances, time, people and accountability.

    Luke 16:10
  7. 07

    Communication and Conflict Resolution

    Speaking truth in love and restoring relationships.

    Matthew 18:15
  8. 08

    Leading Through Crisis

    Lessons from Nehemiah and Daniel under pressure.

Apostolic and Prophetic Vision

    Ephesians 2:20

    The Five-fold Ministry

  3. 03

    Apostolic Grace

    Pioneering, building, establishing and sending.

    Acts 13:2–3
  4. 04

    Prophetic Grace

    Seeing, declaring and aligning the Church with God’s purpose.

    Amos 3:7
  5. 05

    Apostolic and Prophetic Partnership

    How the two graces work together.

    Acts 11:27–30
  6. 06

    Church Planting and Kingdom Expansion

    Building what the gates of hell cannot overcome.

    Matthew 16:18
  7. 07

    Reaching the Nations

    Missions and cross-cultural ministry.

    Matthew 28:19
  8. 08

    Order, Covering and Accountability

    Doing all things decently and in order.

    1 Corinthians 14:40
